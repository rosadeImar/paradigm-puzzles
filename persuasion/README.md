# Persuasion

`description.txt` is our entry on the [Persuasion board](https://www.paradigm.xyz/puzzles/persuasion):
a **$200** median price at 135 characters (entry `99a1f4b1-5e0e-487f-99dc-07dae3cd5d5c`,

it into this one.

**Where we stopped.** The text names the pen, its resin, clip and 14K gold nib, gives a retail
price, an asking price and a buyback at the asking price, and says it is new in its box with
papers. Variants of it have not priced higher.
